About Giatros

Giatros is a healthcare appointment platform that connects patients with doctors. The system involves two primary agents, each with distinct roles and capabilities.

Patient Agent

A Patient is a registered user who is seeking medical care. Patients can:

  • Search for doctors by specialization, insurance, or location
  • Browse doctor profiles and ratings
  • Schedule appointments with available doctors
  • View and manage their upcoming appointments
  • Maintain a personal health profile

To become a patient, create an account and sign in to access the full suite of patient features.

Doctor Agent

A Doctor is a registered medical practitioner listed on the platform. Doctors can:

  • Maintain a public profile with their specialization and accepted insurance
  • List their organization and contact details
  • View all upcoming patient appointments
  • Manage appointment schedules

Doctors are registered by the platform administrators and are associated with a verified user account.

How the Agents Interact

The Giatros platform acts as an intermediary that facilitates communication and scheduling between patients and doctors:

  1. A Patient searches for a doctor based on location, specialization, or accepted insurance.
  2. The platform returns a list of matching Doctor profiles.
  3. The Patient selects a doctor and schedules an appointment for a preferred date and time.
  4. The Doctor can review all upcoming appointments through their dashboard.
  5. Both agents can manage their profiles to keep information current.
